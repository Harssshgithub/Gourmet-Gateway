Upgrade the existing website with premium 3D motion, cinematic animations, and high-end UI improvements.

IMPORTANT:
The website should NOT look like a generic AI-generated or “vibe-coded” website.
Avoid:
- Random animations with no purpose
- Overuse of gradients
- Generic UI blocks
- Template-like layouts
- Overcrowded effects
- Cheap glassmorphism everywhere

Instead, focus on:
- Clean, intentional design
- Strong layout hierarchy
- Premium spacing and alignment
- Minimal but meaningful animations
- Real luxury brand feel (like high-end hotel or fine-dining websites)

Project:
Amrithya – The Eternal Dining by Brahmastra

Goal:
Create a cinematic, immersive restaurant website that feels like a real luxury brand experience and converts visitors into reservations.

---

🎬 Visual Direction:
- Dark luxury theme with controlled lighting
- Gold accents used subtly (not overpowering)
- Soft reflections and ambient glow
- Inspired by premium hospitality brands (not flashy UI kits)
- High-quality composition and balance

---

🎥 Motion & Interaction:
- Smooth scroll-based storytelling (no jittery effects)
- Subtle parallax depth in hero section
- Very light floating particles (barely noticeable)
- Elegant fade/slide transitions between sections
- Micro-interactions on buttons and cards
- Menu cards with refined hover effects (no gimmicks)
- Gallery with controlled 3D tilt (minimal, smooth)

Avoid:
- Over-animated elements
- Fast or distracting motion
- “Gaming-style” 3D effects

---

🧠 UX Focus:
- Clear user journey:
  Hero → Experience → Menu → Gallery → Reservation
- Strong CTA visibility (Reserve Table)
- Clean navigation
- Fast-loading experience

---

🎯 Hero Section:
- Cinematic feel (like a luxury brand landing page)
- Subtle motion (slow zoom or light parallax)
- Headline:
  “Amrithya – The Eternal Dining”
- Subheadline:
  “Where ambience, flavour, and timeless moments come together.”
- CTA buttons:
  Reserve a Table / Explore Menu

---

📦 Sections to Enhance:
- Experience section (premium storytelling layout)
- Signature spaces (clean card/grid design)
- Menu preview (structured and readable)
- Gallery (high-end aesthetic layout)
- Reservation form (minimal, elegant, trustworthy)
- Testimonials (clean and believable)
- Contact section (clear and functional)

---

🎨 Design System:
- Colors:
  Deep Black (#0B0B0B)
  Charcoal (#1A1A1A)
  Antique Gold (#C9A14A)
  Warm Amber (#D6A85A)
  Ivory (#F5F2EB)

- Typography:
  Elegant serif for headings (luxury feel)
  Clean sans-serif for body

- Layout:
  Grid-based
  Consistent spacing
  No clutter

---

⚙️ Tech & Implementation:
- Use React + Tailwind
- Use Framer Motion for subtle animations
- Optional: React Three Fiber ONLY if it enhances experience (keep it light)
- Prioritize performance and smoothness

---

🚀 Final Outcome:
The website should feel like:
- A real premium restaurant brand
- A high-end hospitality experience
- Designed by a professional agency

NOT like:
- A template
- A beginner project
- A generic AI-generated “cool looking” site
